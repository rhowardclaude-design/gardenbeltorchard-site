Rental-property software, built by people who rent homes.

Garden Belt Orchard keeps a small rental business running smoothly: bookings, calendars, guest messages, cleaning turnovers, supplies, and repairs, all in one place.

What Garden Belt Orchard does

Bookings and calendars

One calendar for every home, kept in step with Airbnb and Vrbo so a night is never booked twice.

Guest messages

Booking confirmations, check-in instructions, and check-out reminders go out on time, and every guest text lands in one shared inbox.

Cleaning and supplies

Crew schedules, turnover photos, and monthly supply counts, so each home is ready for the next guest.

Repairs and projects

Track vendors, costs, and milestones for every repair or renovation, and block the calendar while work is underway.

Garden Belt Properties

Garden Belt Properties owns and operates furnished short-term and long-term rentals in Worcester, Massachusetts: three apartments and a house. Garden Belt Properties uses Garden Belt Orchard to manage its homes and to keep in touch with guests.

Texts to our guests

Guests who give us their mobile number for a reservation receive text messages about their stay: a booking confirmation, check-in instructions, a check-in follow-up, check-out reminders, and replies to their questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our text messaging terms and privacy policy.
