Text messaging terms

Last updated October 5, 2026

These terms cover text messages (SMS and MMS) sent by Garden Belt Properties through Garden Belt Orchard. Both are brands of American Energy Consultants, Inc., 210 Park Ave, Unit 314, Worcester, MA 01609.

Who receives texts

  • Guests with a reservation at one of our Worcester homes, about that stay.
  • Our cleaning crew and vendors, about work schedules, cleanings, supply counts, and repair jobs.

How you opt in

  • Guests: you opt in when you give us your mobile number for your reservation and agree to receive texts about your stay, or when you text us first. Texts are not required to book; if you prefer, we can send your stay information by email instead.
  • Crew and vendors: after you are added to our team, we send one text asking you to reply YES. We don't send work texts until you do.

What you'll receive

Guests receive messages about their stay only:

  • a booking confirmation,
  • check-in instructions the day before arrival (which may include door codes, Wi-Fi, and parking details),
  • a check-in follow-up asking whether you need anything,
  • a check-out reminder the day before you leave,
  • replies to your questions, and notices about your stay (for example, a maintenance visit).

Some replies to your questions may be drafted with the help of AI and are sent by our team.

We don't send marketing or promotional texts.

Examples

[Garden Belt Properties] Hi Sam! Your stay at Garden Belt Apt 1 is confirmed for Oct 10 - Oct 12 (2 nights). We'll send your check-in instructions before you arrive. Questions? Just reply to this message. Reply STOP to opt out.
[Garden Belt Properties] Hi Sam, we hope you're settling in at Garden Belt Apt 1! Is there anything you need? Just reply to this text and we'll take care of it.

How often

Message frequency varies. Guests usually receive 4 to 6 automatic texts per stay, plus any conversation you start with us. Crew members receive up to two schedule texts a day while they are on our team, plus occasional texts about supply counts and repair jobs.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. We don't charge for our texts.

Opting out and getting help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. We'll confirm once and send nothing further. Reply START to receive texts again.

Reply HELP for help, or contact us at hello@gardenbeltorchard.com or 508-864-7884.

If you opt out, you can still reach us by email or phone, and we'll send stay information by email when we have your address.

Carriers

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

We use your number only to send the messages described above. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or text-message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. See our privacy policy for details.
