Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

This policy explains how American Energy Consultants, Inc., doing business as Garden Belt Orchard and Garden Belt Properties ("we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects personal information. It covers this website, our rental homes, the messages we send, and the Garden Belt Orchard software.

Information we collect

  • Guests: your name, email address, mobile number, reservation details (dates, home, number of guests), and the messages and photos you send us. If you book through Airbnb or Vrbo, we receive reservation details from that platform.
  • Crew and vendors: your name, contact details, work assignments, and the photos and notes you send about cleanings, supplies, and repairs.
  • Software users: your name, email address, sign-in details (passwords are stored only as secure hashes), and the information you enter.
  • Payments: if you pay online, your card is processed by our payment provider. We don't store full card numbers.

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and doesn't track you across other sites.

How we use it

  • to manage reservations and give you access to your home (for example, sending check-in instructions and door codes),
  • to answer your questions and look after you during your stay,
  • to schedule cleanings, supplies, and repairs,
  • to keep our homes and systems safe and to prevent fraud,
  • to meet legal, tax, and insurance requirements.

Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and agree to texts, we use it only for the messages described in our text messaging terms. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except with the service providers that deliver our messages on our behalf.

AI-assisted replies

To answer guests quickly, our software may use an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to draft replies to guest messages. The guest's message and relevant stay details are sent to the AI provider to create the draft. The provider processes this information on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. Our team reviews drafts, and routine answers may be sent automatically.

Who we share information with

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, under agreements that limit how they use it:

  • Twilio (text messages),
  • Postmark (email),
  • Anthropic (AI-drafted replies, as described above),
  • our hosting and storage providers,
  • Airbnb and Vrbo, when you book through them,
  • our payment provider, when you pay online.

We may also share information when the law requires it, to protect people or property, or as part of a sale or reorganization of our business.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted in transit. Door codes and Wi-Fi passwords are encrypted when stored, and copies of messages keep codes masked. Access is limited to the people who need it, and every sign-in to our software requires a secure password, with two-step verification for owners and administrators.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we need it to provide your stay or service, to resolve disputes, and to meet legal, tax, and insurance requirements. Then we delete or anonymize it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct, or delete your personal information by emailing hello@gardenbeltorchard.com. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it. You can stop texts at any time by replying STOP.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. Reservations must be made by adults.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date.
